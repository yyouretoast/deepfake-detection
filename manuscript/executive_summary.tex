\noindent\textbf{1. Problem Formulation \& Practical Gaps}\\
Contemporary deepfake detectors achieve high in-distribution accuracy ($>99\%$) but fail in real-world deployments due to two distinct vulnerabilities:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Identity Memorization}: Conventional benchmarks use random train/test splits that cross-contaminate actor identities. Models memorize human faces rather than manipulation artifacts, producing severe false alarms on unseen subjects.
    \item \textbf{Compression Fragility}: Existing frequency-domain detectors associate high-frequency perturbations with forgery without measuring signal quality. Standard social media re-compression strips high frequencies, causing detector failure.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1.0ex}
\noindent\textbf{2. Proposed Multi-Modal Architecture}\\
We formulate a physically grounded forensic architecture that targets manipulation traces across spatial, spectral, and temporal domains:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Spatial Stream (ConvNeXt-Small)}: Extracts 512-dimensional structural embeddings, using $7\times 7$ depthwise separable convolutions to isolate facial illumination inconsistencies and blending boundary seams.
    \item \textbf{Spectral Stream (\texttt{ResSE-Spectral})}: Processes a 20-channel forensic representation combining 9 Spatial Rich Model (SRM) filters, 1 learnable Bayar-Stamm prediction-error filter, and 2D Fourier magnitude and phase spectra to detect sensor noise anomalies and generative upsampling artifacts.
    \item \textbf{SNR-Adaptive Gating}: Continuously monitors input noise power to dynamically balance spatial and spectral features. Under heavy compression or blur, the gate automatically dampens frequency representations and delegates decision weight to the spatial stream.
    \item \textbf{Spatiotemporal Video Head}: A two-layer Bidirectional GRU modeling feature velocity deltas alongside temporal attention and max-pooling, preventing localized 1-frame manipulation glitches from being diluted across long videos.
    \item \textbf{Probability Calibration \& Triage}: Applies post-hoc Platt temperature scaling to suppress deep learning overconfidence, reducing Expected Calibration Error (ECE) by \textbf{49.4\%} and establishing an operational 3-zone Bayesian triage policy (Authentic Clearance, Human Review, Confirmed Synthetic).
\end{itemize}
